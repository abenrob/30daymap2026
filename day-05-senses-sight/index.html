What you can see from every point of the GR 738 across Belledonne

The Haute Traversée de Belledonne runs 131 km from Aiguebelle to Vizille. Each frame is a viewshed from eye height (1.7 m) on the trail, about every 150 m: amber is the ground in sight at that step, the faint tint is everything already seen. 900 runs of gdal_viewshed on IGN RGE ALTI at 50 m, with Earth curvature and refraction and no distance cap.
