30DayMapChallenge 2026 · Day 9 · Urban-rural

Same density, different class: two towns 8 km apart on the line from Paris to Chartres

One straight line from Notre-Dame to Chartres cathedral, 78 km, averaged over a 3 km wide swath every kilometre. It is traced in turn by population, sealed ground and built volume, then coloured by the official Degree of Urbanisation class from GHS-SMOD. The finale lays the three measures over the class. Open the interactive version to scrub through it.

The class is not a density threshold on its own. The method classes 1 km cells by density and by the cluster they are contiguous with, so Jouy-en-Josas and Magny-les-Hameaux, both near 1,400 people per km² in the swath, land on either side of a class boundary.
