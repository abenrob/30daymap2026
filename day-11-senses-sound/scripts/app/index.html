Day 11 · Senses: Sound

Seattle Sound,
1988–1992

For four years a handful of Seattle rooms, most of them a short walk apart downtown, hosted the shows that turned a local sound into a global one.

Sub Pop, the label Bruce Pavitt and Jonathan Poneman ran from downtown, put Mudhoney, Nirvana, Soundgarden and TAD on record and on stage. Mother Love Bone's collapse led to Pearl Jam. Alice in Chains came up through the same rooms.

Afterwards

The sound outgrew the rooms

By the end of 1992 the Seattle sound belonged to everyone. The rooms stayed local: the OK Hotel closed after the 2001 Nisqually earthquake and is now apartments with artist studios.
