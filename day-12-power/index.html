Modelled, not metered

No operator publishes hourly flows for every line, so these are modelled with a DC power flow on the real network topology. The inputs are measured: hourly demand and generation by fuel for each country, and the physical flow across each border. Each country's net export matches the measured border totals; how that flow splits across parallel lines, and anything deep inside a country, depends on where the model places demand and generation, and is indicative only.

A DC power flow ignores voltage and reactive power, and the blackout was a voltage event. This map shows where active power went before, during and after. It says nothing about why the grid fell, and makes no claim about cause or responsibility. For that, read the ENTSO-E expert panel report.

Hourly averages hide the collapse itself, which took seconds at 12:33 CEST. The 12:00 hour blends the last normal half hour with the first minutes of the blackout.
