Là où l'on a peur, là où ça va mieux: what Grenoble cyclists wrote on the map

The FUB's Baromètre des villes cyclables asks cyclists to drop points on a map: red where something needs fixing first, green where it improved in the last two years. In Grenoble-Alpes-Métropole the 2025 edition collected 13,510 points, 8,359 with a comment. Each comment was read and tagged with one feeling and a valence; the text overrides the layer when the two disagree (238 points). The map then bins every point into H3 hexagons of about 400 m: hue is the share of positive points, strength is how many points there are. Run the scene live in your browser.

A self-selected survey: a red point means someone wanted it fixed. Feelings are a model's reading of short, often ironic comments. The order the points fall in is random; the points have no dates.
